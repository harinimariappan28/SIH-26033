## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
FARMLOOP is a digital platform that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers and other bulk buyers.

Farmers can list their products, quantities and expected prices. Buyers can browse available products and place orders directly.

The platform also provides:

Direct farmer-to-buyer connections.

Group selling for small-scale farmers.

Shared transportation facilities.

Market price information.

AI-based demand prediction.

Delivery and order tracking.

This helps reduce unnecessary intermediaries, improve farmers' selling opportunities and make products more affordable for consumers.

## Technical Approach
1.FarmerFARMLOOP uses a web-based application supported by a database and intelligent features.Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the user interface.
Backend: Python Django for handling users, products and orders.Database: MySQL for storing farmer details, product listings and order information.
AI Integration: Demand prediction to identify popular products and estimate future requirements.Route Optimization: Location-based route planning to coordinate efficient product delivery.

Working Process:

1. register and upload their products.
2.Buyers browse products and place orders.
3.The system matches buyers with suitable farmers.
4.Nearby farmers can combine products for bulk orders.
5.Shared transportation is arranged.
6.Buyers receive their products directly.

## Feasibility and Viability
Technical Feasibility:

Can be developed using commonly available technologies.

A basic version can be built using Python, Django and MySQL.

AI and route optimization can be added gradually.

Economic Feasibility:

Reduces individual transportation expenses through shared delivery.

Does not require owning warehouses or vehicles initially.

Can generate revenue through small service fees or delivery coordination charges.

Operational Feasibility:

Simple interface suitable for farmers and buyers.

Regional language support can improve accessibility.

Can initially be tested with a small group of farmers and buyers.

Viability:
The platform can expand to different agricultural markets and support more farmers, buyers and transportation providers as the user base grows.

## Impact and Benefits
For Farmers:

Better access to buyers.

Improved selling opportunities.

Reduced transportation expenses.

Greater control over selling prices.

For Consumers:

More affordable agricultural products.

Access to fresh produce.

Better product information.
